BEFORE THE NEXT EVENT

Property hardening is a relationship, not a sales pitch.

Our Property Hardening & Loss-Prevention Walkthrough helps owners understand visible risk areas—including drainage, exterior conditions, roof and water pathways, accessible plumbing concerns, and signs of moisture intrusion.

You receive a prioritized roadmap. If corrective work is needed, you can use your preferred contractor or request help identifying an appropriate provider.

Request a Property Walkthrough

WALKTHROUGH FOCUS

  • Visible water-intrusion indicators
  • Drainage and water-flow concerns
  • Exterior envelope and sealing conditions
  • Accessible plumbing and appliance connections
  • Flood-readiness and vulnerable openings
  • Roofline, trees, and exterior maintenance risks

Walkthroughs address visible and accessible conditions; they are not engineering, code, mold, or insurance-coverage determinations.
